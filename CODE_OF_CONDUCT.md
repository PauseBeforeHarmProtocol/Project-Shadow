# Project Shadow participation code

Project Shadow depends on disciplined disagreement. Participants may challenge
the project, its evidence, its methods, its maintainers, and one another. Such
criticism should be treated as evidence to examine, not hostility to punish.

## Expected conduct

- Be specific about the claim, artifact, path, test, or decision at issue.
- Separate observed facts, interpretations, uncertainty, and recommendations.
- Correct mistakes openly and preserve the evidence needed to understand them.
- Protect personal data, private evidence, credentials, and exploit details.
- Respect different experience levels and make technical reasoning inspectable.
- Stop escalation when harm, coercion, harassment, or unsafe disclosure appears.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention,
doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, knowingly fabricated
evidence, retaliation for good-faith criticism, and publishing exploit details
after being directed to the private security channel are not acceptable.

## Reporting and response

For sensitive conduct reports, email
`pausebeforeharmprotocol_PBHP@protonmail.com` with the subject
`PROJECT SHADOW CONDUCT REPORT`. Do not place private evidence in a public
issue. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or refer a security
matter to the process in `SECURITY.md`. Decisions should be proportionate,
documented where privacy permits, and open to correction when new evidence
appears.

This code governs participation only. It does not certify the project or
authorize production or operational deployment.

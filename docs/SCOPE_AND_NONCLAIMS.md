# Scope and nonclaims

## Authorized release scope

The exact R1 archive is released only as:

`PROJECT SHADOW 1.0 / R1 REFERENCE / BETA-ACTIVE-TESTING / PRELIVE`

The exact v0.3.4 sidecar is released separately as an optional, default-off,
external research companion. It is not canonical R1, not an admitted R1
descendant, and not R1-conforming.

## Not authorized or claimed

This public release does not authorize or claim:

- production use;
- operational deployment;
- efficacy;
- safety;
- certification;
- legal compliance;
- third-party rights or trademark clearance;
- affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship, or official status; or
- expansion beyond the exact artifacts and stated scope.

Publishing release-status and download information on the six authorized
websites is a communications update. It is not production or operational
deployment of Project Shadow.

## Integrity is not fitness

Hashes, manifests, deterministic checks, archive scans, signature evidence,
and verifier passes establish only the specific integrity and structural facts
they test. They do not establish real-world performance, safety, efficacy,
fitness for a purpose, certification, or legal compliance.

# Maintainer continuity and stale-state policy

- **Recorded:** 2026-08-15
- **Continuity review due:** 2026-09-13
- **Stale after:** 2026-10-13

This record describes who may speak for Project Shadow if its maintainer is
unavailable. It does not grant operational authority and does not change any
released artifact, tag, admission, authorization, or nonclaim.

## Current authority

Phillip Linstrum is the sole current human authority to issue a new Project
Shadow release decision, correction disposition, revocation, or supersession.
No co-maintainer, alternate release authority, or successor is designated as
of the recorded date.

AI systems, CI jobs, verifiers, test results, repository accounts, signatures,
receipts, and other automated or evidentiary mechanisms may preserve or report
facts. They may not authorize publication, appoint a successor, issue a
correction disposition, revoke an artifact, or supersede a human governance
decision.

## If the maintainer is unavailable

Unless an explicit successor designation attributable to Phillip Linstrum was
published before the unavailability and states its scope and effective date,
no person or automated system may issue a new Project Shadow release,
correction disposition, revocation, or supersession in the project's name.
Silence, repository access, possession of credentials, contribution history,
or operation of an automated workflow does not create successor authority.

Existing exact-hash releases remain historical public evidence under their
recorded scope and rights boundaries. Maintainer unavailability or staleness
does not silently rewrite, revoke, validate, or expand those releases.

The security mailbox may continue to receive reports, but receipt by a mailbox
or automated acknowledgment is not a governance decision. Public reports must
still follow `SECURITY.md` and must not disclose exploit details or private
evidence.

## Stale state

If this continuity status is not expressly reaffirmed by Phillip Linstrum or
replaced by a valid pre-published successor designation, it is **STALE after
2026-10-13**. Once stale:

- no surface may represent Project Shadow governance, support, corrections, or
  release authority as current;
- no new release, correction disposition, revocation, or supersession may be
  attributed to Project Shadow; and
- existing releases may be described only as preserved historical artifacts
  with their exact published scope and nonclaims.

Staleness is a fail-closed continuity state. It is not evidence that an exact
release is invalid, and it is not production or operational authorization.
